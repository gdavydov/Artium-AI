## 7. Data model change: Organization + CollectionAccess (security)

Following a design discussion about multi-tenancy and private-collection
visibility:

- Added `organization` table (museum/institution: `name`, `description`,
  `website_url`, `contact_email`, `address`, audit timestamps).
- Made `collection.organization_id` a required FK — one Organization owns
  many Collections.
- Added `collection_access` join table (`collection_id`, `user_id`,
  `access_level` — `view`/`manage`, `granted_by`, `granted_at`, unique on
  `(collection_id, user_id)`) to grant specific users access to specific
  private Collections.
- Security rule (documented as an application-layer rule, not a DB
  constraint): **Admins always see everything**, regardless of
  `Collection.type` or any access grant. Public Collections are visible
  to all staff and anonymous visitors. Private Collections are visible
  only to Admins and users with a matching `collection_access` grant.
  `manage` access is additive to the existing Role permission matrix, not
  a replacement.
- Updated `schema.sql`, `schema.prisma`, the Design Document (`.md` +
  `.docx` — entity summary, relationships, new Sections 2.4.1/2.4.2,
  permission matrix, all ER diagrams, new Appendix A.12
  Organization / A.13 CollectionAccess), the Business Requirements
  Document, and regenerated `full-erd.png` again.
